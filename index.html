The Atelier

A private practice in home craft & digital engineering.

Job's Crest Holdings Limited is a small, deliberately selective studio of licensed tradespeople and software engineers — plumbers, solar experts, decorative painters, and developers — working under one roof. Fewer clients, deeper attention, obsessively finished work.

Since 2009 we've quietly maintained homes and built digital platforms for architects, collectors, and private clients across the region. Our work is rarely photographed. It's simply lived in.
